Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 Nov 2026

VYRO is a free Windows app. It has no account, no sign-up and no ads, and it does not sell or share your data.

What stays on your PC

Your settings, profiles, test results, session history and backups are saved only on your own computer. They are never uploaded.

What VYRO connects to

  • The ping target you choose: to measure ping, jitter and packet loss.
  • Cloudflare's speed test (speed.cloudflare.com): only when you run the ping-under-load test.
  • DNS servers: only when you run the DNS speed comparison.
  • GitHub: to check for new versions and download updates.

These services see your internet address, as any website does. VYRO sends them no personal information.

Games

VYRO only reads the names of running programs (the same list Task Manager shows) to spot a game. It never reads, changes or injects into games or anti-cheat software.

This website

This site uses no tracking or analytics cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and fonts load from Google Fonts. Both may log basic visit details, such as your internet address.
